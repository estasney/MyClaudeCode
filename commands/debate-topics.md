Analyze the content in $ARGUMENTS and identify key topics that warrant debate or deeper discussion. For each topic, write:

1. **Topic Name**: Clear, concise title
2. **Position Statement**: Your initial stance/recommendation 
3. **Key Questions**: 2-3 specific questions that need resolution
4. **Reply Section**: Empty section marked with "> Reply:"

Format each topic as:

## {Topic Name}
> **{Position Statement}**: [Your detailed position]
> 
> Key questions:
> - [Question 1]
> - [Question 2]
> - [Question 3]

> Reply:

Focus on topics where there are multiple valid approaches, trade-offs to consider, or implementation details that need clarification.
